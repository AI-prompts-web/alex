My story

From curiosity
to creating things.

I like understanding how things work — and then finding ways to make them work better.

My interest in technology started with a simple curiosity: how can a few lines of code become something people use every day? That question led me into software development, where I found the perfect mix of logic, creativity, and continuous learning.

Over the years, I've worked across frontend interfaces and backend systems, helping turn early ideas into useful digital products. I enjoy the whole process: understanding a problem, exploring possible solutions, building carefully, and refining the details after real people start using the product.

Today, I’m based in Copenhagen, Denmark. I value thoughtful collaboration, clear communication, and teams that care about the quality of what they put into the world.
