How I work

Good work is built on good decisions.

From the first sketch to the final deployment, I focus on understanding the problem before choosing the technology. The goal is always a solution that feels intuitive and works reliably.

01 — Understand

Clarify the goals, audience, constraints, and what success should look like.

02 — Plan

Shape the architecture and map out a practical, maintainable approach.

03 — Build

Develop in focused steps, paying attention to quality and usability.

04 — Refine

Test, improve, and polish the details before delivery.
